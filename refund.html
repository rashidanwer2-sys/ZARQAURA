CUSTOMER CARE

Refund Policy

We want every ZARQAURA order to reach you exactly as expected. This policy explains how we handle returns, exchanges, cancellations, damaged items and refunds.

ZARQAURA POLICY

We’re here to help.

If there is a problem with your order, please contact ZARQAURA as soon as possible with your order ID and details of the issue. Please do not send an item back without first contacting us and receiving confirmation.

01

Return Eligibility

A return or replacement may be considered when an item received is damaged, defective, incorrect or materially different from the item ordered.

Items submitted for return should remain unused, unworn and in their original condition with the original packaging.

02

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your jewellery arrives damaged, defective or you receive an incorrect item, contact us promptly after delivery.

Please share:

  • Your ZARQAURA order ID.
  • Your name and registered phone number.
  • Clear photos or videos showing the item and issue.
  • A short description of what went wrong.
03

Condition of Returned Items

Jewellery approved for return should be sent back in the condition in which it was received.

Products that have been worn, used, altered, intentionally damaged or returned without their original packaging may not be eligible for a refund or replacement.

04

Exchanges

Where an exchange is approved, replacement is subject to product availability.

If the same item is not available, we will communicate the available resolution with you before proceeding.

05

Refunds

Once an eligible returned item has been received and reviewed, we will inform you about the status of your refund.

Approved refunds will be processed using the applicable payment method or another mutually agreed method.

After a refund is initiated, the time taken for the amount to appear may depend on your bank or payment provider.

06

Order Cancellations

If you wish to cancel an order, please contact us as soon as possible and provide your order ID.

We will try to accommodate cancellation requests before an order is dispatched. Once an order has been packed, dispatched or handed to the delivery partner, cancellation may no longer be possible.

07

Return Shipping

Return instructions will be shared after your request has been reviewed and approved.

Please do not courier or ship jewellery to any address without receiving return instructions from ZARQAURA first.

08

Need Help?

For any return, refund, cancellation or order-related issue, contact us through our official WhatsApp or email.

Please include your order ID whenever possible so we can assist you faster.
