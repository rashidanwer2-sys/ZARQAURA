Refund Policy

Current policy: We have a 30-day return policy, which means you have 30 days after receiving your item to request a return.

Eligibility

To be eligible for a return, the item must be in the same condition that you received it, unworn or unused, with tags, and in its original packaging. You will also need the receipt or proof of purchase.

Starting a return

Contact us at info@zarqaura.com. Returns need to be sent to ZARQAURA, Long Island, Pride World City, Charholi Budruk, Pune, Maharashtra 412105, India. Items sent back without first requesting a return will not be accepted.

Damages and issues

Please inspect your order upon reception and contact us immediately if the item is defective, damaged or if you receive the wrong item.

Exceptions

Custom products, sale items and gift cards may not be eligible for return. Contact us if you have questions about a specific item.

Refunds

Once the return is received and inspected, we will let you know if the refund was approved. If approved, the refund will be processed to the applicable payment method. Bank or payment-provider processing times may vary.
