YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information may be provided when you browse, shop or contact ZARQAURA and how that information is used.

ZARQAURA PRIVACY

Simple, respectful handling of your information.

When you interact with ZARQAURA, certain information may be required to help you place an order, receive your jewellery, complete payment or contact us for support. We aim to request only the information necessary for these purposes.

01

Information You Provide

When placing an order or contacting ZARQAURA, you may provide personal information such as:

  • Your full name.
  • Phone or WhatsApp number.
  • Email address.
  • Delivery address.
  • Landmark, city, state and PIN code.
  • Your order details and product selections.
  • Information you voluntarily send through WhatsApp, email or other contact methods.
02

How We Use Your Information

Information you provide may be used for purposes connected with your interaction with ZARQAURA, including:

  • Preparing and confirming your order.
  • Arranging delivery to the address provided.
  • Communicating with you regarding your order.
  • Providing customer support.
  • Handling refunds, returns, exchanges or cancellations.
  • Resolving payment or delivery-related queries.
03

Shopping Cart & Browser Storage

The ZARQAURA website may use your browser's local storage to remember information needed for basic shopping functions.

This may include:

  • Products added to your cart.
  • Product quantities.
  • Certain locally generated website information required for the checkout experience.
Important: Browser local storage remains on the device and browser being used unless it is cleared or otherwise removed.
04

Checkout Information

Information entered on the checkout page is used to prepare your order details and make it easier to share the order with ZARQAURA.

The website may prepare a WhatsApp message containing your order ID, selected products, order amount and the delivery information you entered.

You remain in control of whether you send that prepared message through WhatsApp.

05

Payments

Where a UPI payment option is available, the website may create a UPI payment request containing information such as:

  • ZARQAURA's configured UPI ID.
  • The order amount.
  • Your order reference or order ID.

The actual payment is completed through the UPI application or payment service selected on your device.

ZARQAURA's website does not request your UPI PIN, banking password or card PIN.
06

WhatsApp & External Services

Some website actions may open third-party services such as WhatsApp, UPI applications, Instagram or email applications.

Once you leave the ZARQAURA website and interact with an external service, information handled by that service is subject to its own privacy practices and terms.

07

Information Sharing

Customer information may be used or shared only where reasonably necessary to process an order, provide delivery, assist with customer support or meet applicable legal requirements.

For example, delivery-related information may need to be provided to a courier or delivery partner so that an order can reach you.

ZARQAURA does not sell customer personal information.
08

Payment Screenshots

Customers may choose to share a payment screenshot as proof of payment.

Before sending a screenshot, please make sure it does not unnecessarily display sensitive financial information beyond what is required to identify the payment.

09

Data Security

We take reasonable care when handling information provided for orders and customer support.

However, no internet-based communication or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Customers should also protect access to their own devices, email accounts, WhatsApp accounts and payment applications.

10

Children's Information

The ZARQAURA website is intended for customers purchasing jewellery and is not designed specifically to collect personal information from children.

If you believe personal information has been submitted inappropriately, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

11

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the ZARQAURA website, ordering process, payment methods or services change.

The version displayed on this page represents the privacy policy currently provided for use of the website.

12

Contact Us

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy or how information relating to your ZARQAURA order is handled, please contact us through WhatsApp or email.

When contacting us about an existing order, sharing your order ID will help us assist you more efficiently.
