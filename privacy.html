Privacy Policy

This website collects only the information you voluntarily provide for enquiries and orders, such as your name, phone number, email address, delivery address and the items you want to purchase.

How order information is used

Checkout details are used to prepare your order summary and open WhatsApp with the same information so you can send it directly to ZARQAURA. The cart and most order state are stored locally in your browser. WhatsApp is a third-party service and its own privacy terms apply when you use it.

Payment screenshots

When you choose a payment screenshot, the site attempts to use your device’s native share functionality. The website itself does not upload the image to a server. If native file sharing is unavailable, the site opens WhatsApp so you can attach the screenshot manually.
